# Cow Come fusion-style specification

## Authority

This style specification was reverse-engineered from two accepted transformations. The accepted outputs are the highest visual authority:

- `../assets/fusion-target-orange-seated.png`
- `../assets/fusion-target-yellow-gesture.png`

The written rules preserve the diagnostic conclusions from the original source-to-output comparisons; the source photographs are not required during generation. Together, these outputs and the specification define an authored hybrid: source-led composition plus Cow Come species and crude rendering. Older baseline references may supply local motifs, but they do not override them.

## Transformation split

### Preserve from the source

- subject count, broad quadrant, scale, camera height, pose, gesture, and visible-body range;
- hair silhouette and endpoint, glasses, brow attitude, gaze, clothing color, and one additional identity cue;
- negative-space pattern and major left/right weight;
- pose-supporting furniture and held objects;
- three to six landmark groups that keep the place recognizable after aggressive reduction;
- broad color cues that matter to identity, such as white or purple clothing, dark green chairs, orange-brown chair frames, or an orange-edged phone.

Do not require pixel alignment. When translating a wide photograph to 4:3 or another ratio, enlarge, crop, compress, or shift broad masses while preserving spatial logic.

### Transform

- Human skin becomes one saturated yellow/orange cow-hide color.
- Human anatomy becomes a few thick rough masses while retaining source pose mechanics.
- Human head becomes a frontal cow-human with ears, horns, flat eyes, and compact separate muzzle.
- Clothing becomes one broad nearly seamless matte shell or graffiti-painted color field, with at most one to three oversized uneven patches when a source pattern is identity-defining.
- Delete 60–80% of environmental detail and rebuild only a few landmark groups.
- Vegetation becomes stems, discs, ovals, bent strips, or one hedge mass.
- Architecture becomes boxes and roof slabs while preserving a minimal skyline/depth rhythm.
- Furniture becomes rods and planes.
- Source weather and exposure never control the render. Source sky color may remain as a shifted palette anchor, or the sky may become the single invented flat, photographic-cloud, or tie-dye/轧染 field.
- Photographic light is discarded; each object keeps only its own local diffuse shading and no physical relationship to neighbors.
- Every source material is discarded and regenerated. Preserve identity and composition cues, never original photographic surface appearance or unchanged local pixels.

## Whole-frame material conversion

Cow Come is a reconstruction, not a selective style filter. Before rendering, mentally strip every retained subject and landmark to identity, silhouette, placement, function, dominant color, and one or two recognition cues. Rebuild all remaining visual information.

- Apply the same crude primitive and low-frequency plaster language to foreground subjects, background scenery, animals, plants, and hero objects. A realistic island inside a simple cute frame is a hard failure.
- Never preserve a source crop and merely add clay roughness, posterization, painterly noise, or a new background. Resculpt exact contours and small features; modest asymmetry and changed proportions are desirable when identity remains readable.
- Animal fur becomes broad matte masses. Animal eyes become flat inserted shapes with matte pupils; remove irises, catchlights, wet corneas, glass depth, lashes, and photographic reflections.
- Plants become sparse rods, paddles, discs, or blunt petals. Remove leaf veins, serration, translucency, realistic soil/gravel, and sharp botanical tips.
- Planters, cups, food, toys, appliances, screens, and other hero objects become a handful of visibly joined primitives with irregular rims, imperfect edges, shallow dents, and hand-daubed markings.
- Glass and metal become opaque or weakly translucent flat masses with one crude highlight patch at most. Fabrics become broad painted slabs or shells. Screens become abstract color fields or blunt icons, never retained UI.
- Selective stretched textures may still be used as authored style anchors, but they must be generic or regenerated for the new scene and visibly degraded. Never copy an unchanged material patch from the source photograph.

## Character construction

### Head and species

The face reads first as cow-human, then as the source person.

- Let head width follow source hair and glasses rather than a mandatory inverted trapezoid.
- Add two sideways leaf ears and two short uneven gray horns emerging through or behind the hair.
- Preserve hair as one chunky shell with broad grooves; lock fringe, part, side volume, bob or long-hair endpoint.
- Preserve glasses as thick simplified frames matching broad source shape and color.
- Eyes are flat white ovals or soft almonds. Pupils are equal matte dark discs/ovals, medium to large, aimed according to source gaze. Slight crude misplacement is allowed, but both must act as a pair.
- Brows are thick flat or shallow strokes following source arch and attitude.
- Muzzle is a compact pale lavender-gray, pink-gray, or dirty-white rounded rectangle/blunt low oval, one quarter to one third of face height. It must not protrude as a pig snout.
- Nose construction is fixed: two shallow elongated slot-like dents sit in the upper half of the muzzle. Use short vertical/diagonal/almond grooves with low contrast and the same material color; never black bean dots, round punched holes, bead nostrils, or a separate nose pad.
- Mouth construction is also fixed: one clearly visible straight or slightly uneven horizontal seam sits separately across the lower third of the muzzle. It must remain visible even when closed and must not merge with either nostril.

Reject human facial anatomy with added horns, realistic cow skulls, huge horse muzzles, pig-like two-hole snouts, missing mouths, wet noses, realistic sockets, eyelids, irises, glossy eyes, catchlights, lashes, or beauty lips.

### Source-specific body

The body is not a universal template.

- Preserve the pose's structural chain and center of gravity: seated pelvis, crossed/open knees, leaning torso, reaching arms, hands around an object, or standing balance.
- Rebuild only visible anatomy from thick low-detail forms with modest disproportion, lumpy transitions, and under-resolved joints.
- Preserve major source masses. The seated male example keeps substantial thighs and hands around a phone. The gesturing female keeps raised arms and a broad seated torso.
- Clothing may merge torso, hips, and some legs into one continuous colored shell when the pose and garment support it. Tops, skirts, shorts, and trousers are silhouette masses rather than simulated fabric. This is an allowed source-specific abstraction, not a rule.
- Keep sleeves and hems only as broad silhouette breaks. Use one flat base color and, only when useful, one to three oversized uneven hand-daubed patches or blunt doodle shapes. Remove denim wash, jean creases, seams, stitching, pockets, waistbands, belt loops, ribbing, wrinkles, zippers, jewelry, weave, realistic drape, logos, and small repeated prints.
- Hands are proportionally small, cute, dirty-white/pale mitten symbols with three to four short rounded partly fused digits. Preserve position, gesture, and grip while reducing anatomy to the minimum readable silhouette. When both hands grip one object, use two small full glove-like hands as in the orange seated target. Reject large dominant hands, fully hide-colored hands, realistic human fingers, knuckles, joints, nails, tendons, palm lines, and skin folds.
- Keep a phone, cup, bag, chair, or other pose-explaining object as one blunt recognizable primitive.

Reject normal polished anatomy and the former compulsory long-trunk, ultra-short-leg child body. The target is a recognizable source pose rebuilt by an earnest beginner.

## Radical scene reduction

Delete roughly 60–80% of environmental information before reconstruction. Keep only three to six landmark groups, counting a repeated family as one.

Possible landmark groups:

- subject plus supporting chair;
- umbrella canopy and pole;
- one empty-chair family;
- planter or table block;
- roof/building band;
- one hedge/tall-grass mass;
- one foreground plant family;
- ground plane;
- one held pose-critical prop.

### Primitive vocabulary

- Building: box blocks with zero to two dark window rectangles each.
- Roofscape: several large slabs/wedges; tile fields collapse into a few gray planes with stretched texture.
- Umbrella: one canopy and one pole.
- Chair: a few rods plus one seat/back plane; imperfect cloning is welcome.
- Planter: one box with a few stems and leaf discs.
- Hedge/tall plants: one or two green masses or several elongated bent strips.
- Tableware cluster: one or two silhouettes unless one cup/plate explains the pose.
- Phone: one dark rectangle with source-color rim.

Large blank fields and missing information are correct. Preserve only the minimum source arrangement needed for recognition. Do not substitute an unrelated fantasy or stock environment.

## Surface construction

Character and clothing should look hand-built in a beginner sculpting program:

- broad bumpy matte surfaces;
- shallow dents and soft ridges;
- irregular edges and obvious primitive joins;
- low-frequency roughness rather than dense noise;
- blunt hair grooves and horn bumps;
- clothing and skin sharing a coarse handmade surface family.
- clothing reading as a simply painted shell rather than a separately tailored, folded, or textured garment.

Environment materials alternate among flat diffuse color, faint texture, and a few stretched low-resolution images. Suitable texture anchors include a cloud plate, roof plane, foliage oval, gray wall, green hill, chair fabric, or thin pole. Do not texture every surface equally.

Avoid polished clay, wax, ceramic, glossy plastic, wet highlights, realistic fur, pores, detailed bark, fabric simulation, clean vector fills, or sophisticated low-poly art direction.

## Disconnected light and no contact

The accepted style forbids one coherent global illumination model.

- Give every major asset only its own cheap local diffuse/form shading.
- Brightness, shading direction, and contrast may disagree among adjacent objects.
- The sky is a backdrop and never relights the scene.
- Preserve weak internal self-shading only: darker turned-away faces, shallow dents, and small creases within one object.
- Disable all cast, projected, receiving, contact, and ground shadows.
- Disable ambient occlusion, contact darkening, bounce light, color bleeding, rim light, atmospheric haze, shared reflection, and physical integration.

The frame should feel like independently previewed assets hard-composited in one viewport, while each object retains enough local volume to remain 3D.

## Hybrid source-anchored color engine

Cute color is desired; cute anatomy and automatic full-scene candy recoloring are not. First extract two to four identity- or place-bearing source anchors, then apply the user's selected strategy. If no strategy is supplied, use **balanced hybrid as the universal default for portraits, animals, objects, interiors, and places**:

1. **Source-led / restrained:** roughly 60–80% of large masses use transformed source anchors; one or two zones receive invented color. Use when the user chooses restrained creativity.
2. **Balanced hybrid / default:** source-derived clothing, hair/fur, roofs, plants, furniture, wood, objects, and neutrals remain legible while one large field and optionally one tiny echo receive complementary candy or tie-dye color. Use this unless the user chooses another level.
3. **Creative-led / bold:** a large blank sky, wall, window, screen, rug, floor patch, or backdrop may become the dominant invented field, but retain at least two or three source anchors and one dirty neutral. Use when the user chooses bold creativity or explicitly requests stronger invention.

Candidate invented accents include candy cyan, butter yellow, coral, dusty pink, lavender, mint, teal, apricot, electric blue, faded magenta, sulfur yellow, cream, and moss green. Transpose, mute, dirty, or clash them according to the source. The warm cow hide remains free, but clothing identity colors and useful gray, brown, green, charcoal, off-white, and pale-wood anchors should not be erased merely to satisfy a candy palette.

Choose at most one large creative color field: sky, window, wall, screen, rug, floor patch, or distant backdrop. It may be flat, a badly mapped low-resolution cloud plate, or a low-resolution two-to-four-color tie-dye/轧染 field with uneven blooms, dragged bands, crushed gradients, and hard seams. Permit at most one small color echo elsewhere. Never spread the same cloud/tie-dye treatment over multiple unrelated surfaces.

Across a batch, vary strategy, source-anchor balance, invented hues, and the creative-field location. Do not repeatedly use lavender-mint-coral-yellow, full-room pastelization, or the same foreground/background distribution. Color preservation never permits photographic texture or coherent source lighting to survive.

Do not add modeled fluffy clouds, volumetric clouds, clean illustration clouds, realistic atmosphere, or sky-driven lighting. Source weather and exposure do not control the render, although source sky color may remain as a shifted or muted palette anchor.

## Failure modes

Reject outputs that:

- copy a target's pose or props instead of the user's source;
- use an unrelated fixed environment or erase the place completely;
- preserve a detailed photoreal background behind a pasted cartoon cow;
- keep more than roughly 40% of source environment detail;
- force all bodies into the old fixed silhouette;
- lose glasses, hair endpoint, gaze, clothing color, gesture, supporting furniture, or held object;
- use fully hide-colored, anatomically human, large, or finely articulated hands instead of small pale mitten symbols with a few short fused digits;
- become plush, toy-like, mascot-cute, polished, cinematic, realistic, clean low-poly, or premium clay animation;
- use a human face with ears/horns or an oversized horse muzzle;
- use a pig-like protruding muzzle, two black bean-dot/button nostrils, or no separate horizontal mouth seam;
- preserve realistic source materials or unchanged local photographic detail in animals, eyes, fur, plants, planters, props, glass, metal, fabric, food, soil, screens, or hero objects, creating a cutout-like realistic island;
- erase the source's two to four useful palette anchors, or mechanically copy all source colors without one authored intervention;
- recolor more than half of unrelated objects candy without a source or compositional reason, make all surfaces share one saturated pastel family, or produce a homogeneous palette-swap scene;
- repeat cloud/tie-dye treatment across multiple unrelated surfaces rather than confining it to one deliberate large field plus at most one small echo;
- restore source-weather lighting coherence or use modeled clouds instead of a flat, cloud-plate, or tie-dye field when a creative sky is chosen;
- let cute colors turn into kawaii eyes, round mascot volume, friendly plush anatomy, or childish illustration polish;
- give neighboring objects shared light, cast/receiving/contact shadows, ambient occlusion, or physical integration;
- over-detail architecture, furniture, vegetation, clothing, hands, or textures.
- give trousers or other clothing denim wash, folds, seams, pockets, waistbands, fabric weave, realistic drape, or dense patterns instead of a flat graffiti-painted mass.
